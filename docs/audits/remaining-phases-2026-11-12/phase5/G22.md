## PH5-G22-001

**Refresh expired multipart upload URLs during offline replay**

Priority: **P1** · status: **OPEN** · type: `CODE_DEFECT` · evidence: `TARGETED_RUNTIME_OBSERVATION`

**Observed / missing:** B01 executes the actual queue module in Chromium/IndexedDB against a loopback provider fixture: after an expired URL, two drains repeat two PUT 403 failures with one intent, zero refresh calls and no completion. The foreground upload component has refresh logic; the offline queue does not.

**Required correction:** Use a shared bounded multipart replay client that refreshes authoritative outstanding-part URLs when eligible, preserves part receipts and handles expired/aborted intents with explicit safe restart. Never reuse stale URLs indefinitely or move a draft to another actor.

**Acceptance:** Expire URLs between parts and across reload/offline intervals; cover signing/provider 403 distinctions, revoked intent, changed tenant and already-completed parts. Resume to verified READY once without losing the original.

**Runtime observations:** `B01` in [API/worker observations](../evidence/probe-results.json) or [browser observations](../evidence/offline-results.json). These are observation IDs, not one test or one independent bug each.
