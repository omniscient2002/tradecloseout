## PH4-G15-001

**Separate prepared submission, verified dispatch and recipient decision**

Priority: **P1** · status: **OPEN** · type: `WORKFLOW_GAP` · evidence: `SOURCE_CONFIRMED`

**Observed / missing:** createSubmission records status SUBMITTED and returns that while recipients are PENDING_DELIVERY and only SUBMISSION_PREPARED is emitted. The pending-recipient state is honest, but no complete dispatch/receipt/reconciliation workflow makes the submission actually delivered.

**Required correction:** Define prepared/queued/accepted-by-provider/delivered/failed/decided states and implement recipient-specific durable delivery with G20 safe templates and receipts. Preserve nonblocking business intent while presenting truthful external delivery status.

**Acceptance:** Test absent provider, accepted-timeout, bounce, resend, crash and receipt replay. UI, audit and recipient links must distinguish prepared intent from delivered or legally accepted handover.
