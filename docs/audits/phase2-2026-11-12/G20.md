### PH2-G20-001 — Enforce safe message projections and approved templates before delivery

**P1 · CODE_DEFECT · OPEN · TARGETED_RUNTIME_OBSERVATION**

Source: [apps/api/src/modules/notifications/notifications.service.ts:24](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/api/src/modules/notifications/notifications.service.ts#L24), [apps/worker/src/notification-delivery.ts:24](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/worker/src/notification-delivery.ts#L24), [apps/worker/src/scheduler.ts:341](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/worker/src/scheduler.ts#L341).

Observations: P18, N01 in the [shared results](evidence/probe-results.json) or [separate G03 results](evidence/g03-results.json). Probe IDs G01..G11 are observation labels, not group/finding IDs.

**Evidence and failure scenario.** P18 retains commercial values, internal comments and a 100-character requirement title. N01 sends the complete arbitrary payload to a loopback gateway. The weekly owner email itself includes retention amounts. This contradicts the explicit message-body restrictions in FR-NOT-013; gateway acknowledgement does not perform redaction.

**Required correction.** Replace generic client-controlled payload delivery with versioned event templates and channel-specific allowlisted projections. Keep prohibited financial totals and internal details behind an authorized in-app link. Reconcile the rupee-at-risk digest intent with FR-NOT-013 through accountable product/privacy review before allowing derived commercial amounts in external bodies. Truncate permitted titles to 60 characters; validate recipient-visible fields and escape output. Apply the same policy to digest and supplier paths before enqueue and at rendering.

**Acceptance checks.** Use synthetic markers for every prohibited field through all producers, retries and email/SMS/manual WhatsApp renderers. Assert no prohibited content reaches provider request, preview, logs or callback records. Verify long/unicode titles and localized escaping; keep internal dashboards useful within permission.

### PH2-G20-003 — Separate gateway acceptance from delivery and reconcile uncertain outcomes

**P1 · CODE_DEFECT · OPEN · TARGETED_RUNTIME_OBSERVATION**

Source: [apps/worker/src/notification-delivery.ts:12](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/worker/src/notification-delivery.ts#L12), [apps/worker/src/scheduler.ts:538](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/worker/src/scheduler.ts#L538).

Observations: N01 in the [shared results](evidence/probe-results.json) or [separate G03 results](evidence/g03-results.json). Probe IDs G01..G11 are observation labels, not group/finding IDs.

**Evidence and failure scenario.** The generic adapter returns true for accepted=true with a matching notification ID; the scheduler immediately labels the row SENT. It has no business-notification attempt/receipt ledger, signed delivery callbacks, bounce reconciliation or unknown-outcome state. The stronger authentication delivery ledger is a different path and cannot certify these messages.

**Required correction.** Persist claim/attempt/acceptance/delivery/failure states with correlated provider IDs and a reviewed channel contract. Reconcile timeout-after-acceptance and crash-after-send safely; use provider deduplication on the stable message identity. Enforce HTTPS for real adapters, reject redirects, bound response size and validate acknowledgements. Keep secrets out of receipts.

**Acceptance checks.** Fixture tests must cover duplicate send/callback, reordered receipts, forged callbacks, timeout after provider acceptance, DB rollback and restart. Obtain real email/SMS delivery/bounce evidence separately. An accepted request must never be presented as confirmed recipient delivery.

### PH2-G20-004 — Add bounded retries, fair claiming and dead-letter recovery to business notifications

**P1 · CODE_DEFECT · OPEN · SOURCE_CONFIRMED**

Source: [apps/worker/src/scheduler.ts:538](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/worker/src/scheduler.ts#L538), [apps/worker/src/notification-delivery.ts:25](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/worker/src/notification-delivery.ts#L25).

**Evidence and failure scenario.** The scheduler selects the oldest 25 queued rows, calls the provider sequentially while holding the database transaction/row locks, and silently continues on every false result or exception. Failed rows retain the same position with no attempts, next-attempt time, failure classification or dead letter. Twenty-five permanently failing email rows can repeatedly consume the batch; up to 25 ten-second network waits occur inside one transaction. PR16 outbox leases do not change this notification table.

**Required correction.** Claim a bounded batch in a short transaction, send outside locks, and settle each leased attempt with fencing. Persist exponential backoff/jitter, permanent versus transient errors, retry ceilings, dead letters and audited redrive. Provide per-tenant/channel fairness, bounded concurrency, rate limits and queue-age/failure metrics.

**Acceptance checks.** Seed more than one batch with poison rows and a healthy later message. Verify bounded delivery latency, restart/lease expiry, provider outage, 429 Retry-After, shutdown and redrive without duplicate delivery or long-lived DB locks. Alert on backlog age and exhausted retries.

### PH2-G20-007 — Persist and consistently apply urgency, quiet hours and expiry

**P2 · CODE_DEFECT · OPEN · SOURCE_CONFIRMED**

Source: [apps/api/src/modules/notifications/notifications.service.ts:37](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/api/src/modules/notifications/notifications.service.ts#L37), [apps/worker/src/scheduler.ts:559](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/worker/src/scheduler.ts#L559).

**Evidence and failure scenario.** The API calculates deferredForQuietHours using isUrgent, but does not persist urgency. **Acceptance checks.** Test exact 22:00/08:00 IST boundaries, UTC dates, allowed urgent security versus nonessential messages, satisfied requirements, closed/suspended projects, revoked grants and expired upload links. API status and eventual queue behavior must agree.

### PH2-G20-009 — Use one qualified missing-work and financial-summary definition

**P2 · CODE_DEFECT · OPEN · SOURCE_CONFIRMED**

Source: [apps/api/src/modules/notifications/notifications.service.ts:92](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/api/src/modules/notifications/notifications.service.ts#L92), [apps/worker/src/scheduler.ts:302](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/worker/src/scheduler.ts#L302).

**Evidence and failure scenario.** The owner-digest endpoint counts mandatory NOT_STARTED requirements without requiring an overdue due date and does not exclude superseded rows. It reads project-level financial columns/site_json fallback; the scheduled digest instead totals active contracts and filters overdue rows. The two surfaces can disagree. Label informational retention exposure accurately. Exclude superseded/non-applicable work and preserve unknown values rather than inventing money at risk.

**Acceptance checks.** Cross-check dashboard, API digest and scheduled safe summary for no due date, future due, superseded obligations, mixed contracts, variations and unknown values. Use values beyond JS safe integer range and verify scope-filtered counts.

## Production acceptance boundary

All required code/workflow items for the approved release scope need fix-SHA regression evidence and independent review on one integrated candidate. Passing the observation harness means the recorded behavior was reproduced; several observations are defects. It does **not** mean those defects are fixed. External/provider/domain/browser/operational gates remain blocked until their real evidence is supplied. No finite source audit guarantees the absence of every future defect. No application change, deployment, approval, merge, production message or destructive operation is performed by this documentation handoff.
