### PH2-G18-001 — Replace contradictory fallbacks with one effective entitlement policy

**P1 · CODE_DEFECT · OPEN · TARGETED_RUNTIME_OBSERVATION**

Source: [apps/api/src/modules/billing/warranty-and-billing.service.ts:554](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/api/src/modules/billing/warranty-and-billing.service.ts#L554), [apps/api/src/modules/projects/projects.service.ts:890](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/api/src/modules/projects/projects.service.ts#L890), [apps/api/src/modules/documents/documents.service.ts:385](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/api/src/modules/documents/documents.service.ts#L385).

Observations: P01, P02, P03 in the [shared results](evidence/probe-results.json) or [separate G03 results](evidence/g03-results.json). Probe IDs G01..G11 are observation labels, not group/finding IDs.

**Evidence and failure scenario.** P01 reports no subscription and maxActiveProjects=0 while activation succeeds using a ten-project fallback. P02 permits an expired ACTIVE subscription; P03 permits a CANCELLED subscription through the same fallback. Subscription DTO defaults (20 seats/100GB/15 projects) differ from activation defaults, and storage filters a different status set.

**Required correction.** Implement one versioned effective-entitlement resolver for org status, subscription state, period/grace boundaries, plan status and explicit trial/no-plan policy. Unknown, cancelled or missing entitlements must follow an approved safe policy without invented Starter allowances. Share it across reads, invites, uploads, activation/reopen and package generation; preserve authorized reads/exports.

**Acceptance checks.** Table-drive missing, TRIAL, ACTIVE, PAST_DUE, SUSPENDED, CANCELLED, expired/future periods and inactive plans at exact boundaries. API/UI limits and every writer must agree; no bad/missing plan may silently increase allowance.

### PH2-G18-002 — Enforce seats on every membership activation under one capacity lock

**P1 · CODE_DEFECT · OPEN · TARGETED_RUNTIME_OBSERVATION**

Source: [apps/api/src/modules/identity/identity.controller.ts:595](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/api/src/modules/identity/identity.controller.ts#L595), [apps/api/src/modules/identity/identity.controller.ts:619](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/7b2b649ecf33b69e8cf1b5753630d82ed89aaf08/apps/api/src/modules/identity/identity.controller.ts#L619).

Observations: P21, G10 in the [shared results](evidence/probe-results.json) or [separate G03 results](evidence/g03-results.json). Probe IDs G01..G11 are observation labels, not group/finding IDs.

**Evidence and failure scenario.** P21 executes the actual PR16 invitation acceptance path and produces two active members on maxSeats=1. PR13 adds a subscription-row lock and blocks the ACTIVE-plan case, but G10 changes that plan to PAST_DUE and the same capacity is bypassed. PR13 has no quota row when status/plan is absent and does not validate period dates. Its fix is not present in the shared PR16 snapshot.

**Required correction.** Integrate the seat correction with the authoritative entitlement resolver and a lock that exists even when no subscription row does. Define invited/active/suspended seat accounting and enforce signup/reactivation/import/admin activation consistently. Serialize plan edits and acceptances, preserve proof recovery on capacity rejection and make retries idempotent.

**Acceptance checks.** Use actual OTP/invitation acceptance with restricted tc_app for at-limit, below-limit, no-plan, expired and grace states; race multiple acceptances, suspension/removal and downgrade. Verify proof retry/reissue semantics, counter reconciliation and no orphaned user or membership on rollback.

### PH2-G18-003 — Reserve storage atomically and treat zero as a real limit

**P1 · CODE_DEFECT · OPEN · TARGETED_RUNTIME_OBSERVATION**

Source: [apps/api/src/modules/documents/documents.service.ts:385](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/api/src/modules/documents/documents.service.ts#L385), [packages/db/src/tenant-tx.ts:203](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/packages/db/src/tenant-tx.ts#L203).

Observations: P04, P05 in the [shared results](evidence/probe-results.json) or [separate G03 results](evidence/g03-results.json). Probe IDs G01..G11 are observation labels, not group/finding IDs.

**Evidence and failure scenario.** P04 creates an upload intent on a zero-byte plan because the limit is tested by truthiness. P05 creates two ten-byte intents on a ten-byte plan: usage only counts READY bytes and pending work reserves nothing. Existing positive quota checks therefore do not stop pending uploads or later scan completion from exceeding capacity.

**Required correction.** Resolve an explicit validated limit, including zero, and reserve pending bytes atomically under an org usage lock. Reconcile reserved/committed bytes on complete, scanner success/failure, abort, expiry, delete and restore. Define safe over-quota recovery after downgrade; do not weaken scanning or immutable object verification.

**Acceptance checks.** Race intents and scanner completions at the ceiling; verify zero/no-plan, dishonest size, retries, interrupted multipart, failed scan, expired intents, delete/restore and plan downgrade. Actual object sizes must settle exactly once and pending reservations must not leak.

### PH2-G18-004 — Align active-project usage with lifecycle and reopening

**P1 · CODE_DEFECT · OPEN · TARGETED_RUNTIME_OBSERVATION**

Source: [apps/api/src/modules/projects/projects.service.ts:205](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/api/src/modules/projects/projects.service.ts#L205), [apps/api/src/modules/projects/projects.service.ts:475](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/api/src/modules/projects/projects.service.ts#L475), [apps/api/src/modules/projects/projects.service.ts:318](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/api/src/modules/projects/projects.service.ts#L318).

Observations: P07 in the [shared results](evidence/probe-results.json) or [separate G03 results](evidence/g03-results.json). Probe IDs G01..G11 are observation labels, not group/finding IDs.

**Evidence and failure scenario.** Activation increments ACTIVE_PROJECTS, while reopen sets ACTIVE without any reservation (P07). Close/suspend contain no corresponding usage transition and archive is not exposed. The counter cannot consistently mean current active projects, and an org can reopen beyond a zero project entitlement.

**Required correction.** Define which lifecycle states consume capacity, including HANDOVER and SUSPENDED, then implement atomic transition deltas/reservations and a reconciliation job against canonical project state. Reopen/resume must use effective limits. Archive must exclude projects without deleting records; retries and rollback must not double-count.

**Acceptance checks.** Exercise activation, failed activation, suspend/resume, handover/close/reopen, cancel/archive and downgrade, both serially and concurrently. Compare counter to real projects after each transition and restart; allow retained read/export access while over quota.

### PH2-G18-005 — Use plan-specific package limits and idempotent consumption

**P1 · CODE_DEFECT · OPEN · SOURCE_CONFIRMED**

Source: [apps/api/src/modules/handover/handover.service.ts:131](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/api/src/modules/handover/handover.service.ts#L131).

**Evidence and failure scenario.** Handover generation calls consumePlanQuota with hardcoded 50n rather than maxPackageGenerationsPerMonth from the plan. The shared counter SQL defect from Phase1 is fixed in PR16, but this policy still allows or blocks the wrong number of generations. Generation failure/retry needs a defined billable-attempt contract.

**Required correction.** Use the shared effective-entitlement contract and explicit IST billing-period semantics. Define whether failed/cancelled attempts count; bind reservations/settlement to durable generation identity so API/worker retries cannot charge twice or release unrelated usage.

**Acceptance checks.** Test plans below and above 50, zero/no-plan, month boundary, identical generation retry, queue timeout, failed artifact validation and concurrent final slots. Inspect actual counter and generation ledger; never create fake PDFs to make a generation appear successful.

### PH2-G18-006 — Complete subscription fulfilment, expiry, grace and dunning safely

**P1 · WORKFLOW_GAP · OPEN · SOURCE_CONFIRMED**

Source: [apps/api/src/modules/billing/billing.service.ts:172](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/api/src/modules/billing/billing.service.ts#L172), [apps/api/src/modules/billing/warranty-and-billing.service.ts:627](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/api/src/modules/billing/warranty-and-billing.service.ts#L627), [packages/db/migrations/0021_razorpay_verified_webhook_inbox.sql:1](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/packages/db/migrations/0021_razorpay_verified_webhook_inbox.sql#L1).

**Evidence and failure scenario.** The raw signed Razorpay webhook is safely recorded for reconciliation and returns processed=false. Automated fulfilment is intentionally disabled; no complete checkout/activation, provider-to-org reconciliation, period expiry/grace/dunning or plan-state scheduler is registered. Receiving a verified webhook is not an active paid subscription.

**Required correction.** Implement a reviewed subscription state machine and server-owned provider/customer/plan mapping with reconciliation, replay/order handling, idempotent ledger transitions and durable dunning events. Coordinate notification delivery and suspended read access. Keep live fulfilment disabled until provider and finance acceptance exists; never trust webhook notes as tenant authority.

**Acceptance checks.** Cover out-of-order/duplicate/forged events, receipt-before-API-return, timeout/unknown payment outcome, refund/cancellation, expiry/grace/reactivation and provider outage. Real provider sandbox acceptance and operations reconciliation must precede live activation.

### PH2-G18-007 — Validate and version plan definitions and entitlement snapshots

**P1 · CODE_DEFECT · OPEN · SOURCE_CONFIRMED**

Source: [packages/db/migrations/0000_complete_v021_schema_and_rls.sql:1190](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/packages/db/migrations/0000_complete_v021_schema_and_rls.sql#L1190), [packages/db/migrations/0000_complete_v021_schema_and_rls.sql:1200](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/packages/db/migrations/0000_complete_v021_schema_and_rls.sql#L1200), [apps/api/src/modules/billing/billing.service.ts:18](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/api/src/modules/billing/billing.service.ts#L18), [apps/api/src/modules/billing/warranty-and-billing.service.ts:583](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/api/src/modules/billing/warranty-and-billing.service.ts#L583).

**Evidence and failure scenario.** plans.limits_json is unrestricted JSON; readers directly use Number/BigInt or defaults. Missing, fractional, negative, string or oversized limits have no shared schema/version policy. Subscriptions join live mutable plan rows, so future plan edits can retroactively change contractual limits/price presentation without an accepted transition.

Keep the existing UNIQUE subscriptions.org_id constraint; this audit withdraws the hypothesis that duplicate org subscription rows are allowed.

**Acceptance checks.** Test malformed JSON, zero, null/missing fields, unsafe numeric sizes, retired offers, plan edits and concurrent downgrade/usage. Verify old subscriptions keep their agreed version until an explicit transition and public plan output matches actual provider offers.

### PH2-G18-008 — Keep financial issuance gated and remove inconsistent tax paths before enabling it

**P1 · CODE_DEFECT · OPEN · SOURCE_CONFIRMED**

Source: [apps/api/src/modules/billing/billing.controller.ts:30](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/api/src/modules/billing/billing.controller.ts#L30), [apps/api/src/modules/billing/billing.service.ts:83](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/api/src/modules/billing/billing.service.ts#L83), [apps/api/src/modules/billing/gst-einvoice-tds.service.ts:45](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/api/src/modules/billing/gst-einvoice-tds.service.ts#L45).

**Evidence and failure scenario.** Tax invoice issuance is disabled by default, which is a required safety control. If enabled, BillingService.createInvoice still hardcodes 1800 basis points, SAC 998315 and an unconditional Rule48 exemption declaration, although another GST service reads configuration. Subscription activation cannot assume these invoice/tax paths are accepted.

**Required correction.** Keep issuance disabled while reconciling the two paths under authoritative tax configuration, validated supplier/buyer/place-of-supply facts, integer amounts and audited numbering. Obtain accountable finance/legal review for applicability and disclosures; coordinate the later finance group instead of treating G18 as tax certification.

**Acceptance checks.** Prove disabled routes fail honestly; after reviewed fixes, exercise configured tax scenarios, numbering concurrency, refunds/TDS reconciliation and downloadable issued documents against approved fixtures. Live legal/provider acceptance is separate from code tests.

### PH2-G18-009 — Expose honest usage, remaining capacity and actionable billing recovery

**P2 · WORKFLOW_GAP · OPEN · SOURCE_CONFIRMED**

Source: [apps/web/src/app/(contractor)/billing/page.tsx:105](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/web/src/app/(contractor)/billing/page.tsx#L105), [apps/api/src/modules/billing/warranty-and-billing.service.ts:554](https://github.com/omniscient2002/tradecloseout/blob/e1b2d9506f2f5d1cbd853a91cade58f8bab3cee4/apps/api/src/modules/billing/warranty-and-billing.service.ts#L554).

## Production acceptance boundary

All required code/workflow items for the approved release scope need fix-SHA regression evidence and independent review on one integrated candidate. Passing the observation harness means the recorded behavior was reproduced; several observations are defects. It does **not** mean those defects are fixed. External/provider/domain/browser/operational gates remain blocked until their real evidence is supplied. No finite source audit guarantees the absence of every future defect. No application change, deployment, approval, merge, production message or destructive operation is performed by this documentation handoff.
